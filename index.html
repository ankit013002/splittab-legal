Purchases and restore

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID or Google Play account. Open SplitTab Plus and choose Restore Purchase after reinstalling or moving to another device on the same store account.

Apple and Google process payments and refunds. SplitTab and RevenueCat never receive your card details.

Your privacy

SplitTab does not require an account. Bill amounts, names, tips, and split calculations remain on your device and are not sent to PetalForm LLC.
