SplitTab

Privacy Policy — Last updated June 20, 2026

SplitTab is a bill-splitting calculator. Your calculations stay entirely on your device. We do not create accounts, we do not see your bill amounts, and we do not sell your data.

What we collect

SplitTab itself collects no personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, location, or any identifying information.

The following third-party services may collect data as described below:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of SplitTab shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. On iOS, you will be asked for permission before any advertising identifier is used (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, AdMob will still show ads but they will be less personalized.

On Android, Google may use your advertising identifier to show relevant ads. You can opt out in your device's Google settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how AdMob handles data.

Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog to collect anonymous, aggregate usage data — for example, which screens are visited and how quickly they load. No bill amounts, tip percentages, or any financial data are ever recorded. Events cannot be linked back to you personally.

See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

If you purchase "Remove Ads," the transaction is handled by Apple or Google. RevenueCat manages your entitlement (whether ads should be shown) using a non-personal device token. RevenueCat does not receive your payment details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Crash reporting (Sentry)

If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Sentry. Reports include the device OS version, app version, and a stack trace. They do not include bill data or any personal information.

What we do not collect

  • Bill amounts or tip percentages you enter
  • Names of people in a split
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location
  • Contacts or any other device data

Children

SplitTab does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app is a simple calculator and collects no personal information from any user regardless of age.

Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to retain or delete. Anonymous analytics data held by PostHog and Sentry follows their respective retention policies.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you have rights regarding personal data. Since SplitTab does not collect personal data, most requests will be resolved by confirming that nothing is held. For third-party data (AdMob, PostHog, Sentry), please contact those services directly.
