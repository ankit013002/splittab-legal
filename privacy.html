SplitTab

Privacy Policy — Last updated July 16, 2026

SplitTab is a bill-splitting calculator provided by PetalForm LLC. Your calculations stay entirely on your device. We do not create accounts, we do not see your bill amounts, and we do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

SplitTab does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or an account. PetalForm LLC does not receive the bill amounts, names, tip percentages, or split calculations you enter.

The following third-party services may collect data as described below:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of SplitTab shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Where required, Google's consent form is shown before ads are requested. On iOS, you may also be asked for permission before the advertising identifier is used through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline tracking, SplitTab requests non-personalized ads.

Google may process device identifiers, coarse location derived from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other advertising data under its policies. Where regional law requires it, you can reopen Google's consent choices from SplitTab Plus > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also manage advertising settings through your device account.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how AdMob handles data.

In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

If you purchase "Remove Ads," the transaction is handled by Apple or Google. RevenueCat manages the entitlement using a randomly generated app-user identifier plus store transaction and product information. RevenueCat does not receive your full card or bank details.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

  • Bill amounts or tip percentages you enter
  • Names of people in a split
  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location
  • Contacts or any other device data

Children

SplitTab is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can delete it.

Data retention

SplitTab stores onboarding state locally on your device. Purchase and advertising providers retain their respective records under their own retention policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data. Contact us to exercise a right concerning information controlled by PetalForm LLC. Advertising consent can also be changed inside the app where the regional privacy-options form is required.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to PetalForm LLC at petalformllc@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy at this URL and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
