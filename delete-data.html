SplitTab data requests

Access and deletion requests for PetalForm LLC

SplitTab does not create user accounts. Bill amounts, names, tip percentages, and split calculations remain on your device and are not sent to PetalForm LLC.

Request access or deletion

  1. Email petalformllc@gmail.com with the subject SplitTab data request.
  2. State whether you are requesting access, correction, or deletion and identify the email address previously used to contact support, if any.
  3. Do not send bill amounts, payment-card details, passwords, purchase receipts, or Apple or Google account credentials.

PetalForm LLC will acknowledge a verifiable request and respond within the period required by applicable law. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request relates to you.

What can be deleted

  • Support emails and related correspondence controlled by PetalForm LLC, unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, tax, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Locally stored onboarding state, which you can remove immediately by deleting SplitTab from your device.

Third-party records

Apple or Google controls store purchases and refunds. RevenueCat manages the anonymous purchase entitlement, and Google AdMob processes advertising data under its own policies. PetalForm LLC cannot delete records those providers must retain, but we will explain the appropriate provider or device control when responding to your request.

You can revisit regional advertising consent choices in SplitTab Plus > Ad Privacy Choices.

Retention

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. Any information not subject to a valid retention requirement is deleted after the request is completed.

For more detail, read the SplitTab Privacy Policy.
